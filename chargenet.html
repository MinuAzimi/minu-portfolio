ChargeNet, sustainable EV charging.

ChargeNet Stations builds and runs EV charging sites for businesses across California. The brand speaks to two audiences at once: property owners deciding whether to host chargers, and the drivers who use them.

The mark joins a leaf and a lightning bolt. The wordmark splits in two colors, CHARGE in deep blue and NET in green, so clean energy reads in the name itself.

Client
ChargeNet Stations
Location
San Jose, CA, with sites across California
Year
2020
Scope
Identity, color, type, brand guidelines, stationery, email, social
Identity

Letterhead and business card. Volt Blue anchors the page edges; the gradient mark carries the green.

Color

Two blues for trust and structure, one green for energy, and Ampere Blue for calls to action. A green gradient runs through the mark and graphics.

Volt Blue#203C6F

Electric Green#27C182

Guidelines

A brand guide covering logo lockups and clear space, primary and secondary palettes, the gradient, and a Satoshi type hierarchy for light and dark backgrounds.

Social

Instagram posts on the benefits for drivers and communities, set in the brand blues and greens.

Email

Outreach to businesses that could host chargers, from the pitch to the benefits to a call to get in touch.
