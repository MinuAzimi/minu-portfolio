Kooch, a shop in Venice.

Kooch is a retail store on Calle dei Saoneri in Venice. The identity sets Persian motifs beside Venetian ones: pomegranates and glazed tiles next to lagoon water and plaster houses.

In Persian, kooch means migration. A swallow, a migratory bird, sits beside the hand-drawn wordmark on everything the shop hands across the counter.

Client
Kooch
Location
Venezia, Italia
Year
2026
Scope
Identity, color, type, illustration, packaging, signage, campaign
Languages
English, Italiano
Mark

One swallow and one wordmark, printed in a single ink so they hold up on stickers, tags and wax.

Color

Five colors pulled from the illustrations: two pomegranate reds, lagoon blue, blush and limewash plaster.

Type

Averia Serif Libre for headlines; its softened edges sit well next to brushwork. Figtree carries dates, addresses and body copy.

Illustration

An oil-textured library of objects from both cities, cut out and reused across print, packaging and social.

Packaging

Natural paper, red cord and wax. The pattern and swallows wrap each purchase; the mark closes it.

Campaign — Yalda

An invitation to Yalda night, the Persian celebration of the longest night of the year. Email and Instagram, written in English and Italian.
