Personal

Drawing, painting and mixed media.

Drawings in ink, pencil and marker, some finished in color digitally or collaged onto paintings and fabric. Ongoing subjects include portraits, quiet interiors and Miu, a cat with a series of her own.

Minu Azimi is a brand designer and illustrator. She came to design through art, with degrees in Illustration from Scuola Internazionale di Comics in Rome and Graphic Design from San José State University.
